About

Alicia R. Welden is a quantum chemist and computational scientist based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Her work connects electronic structure theory, quantum computing for chemistry, high-performance computing and emerging technology strategy.

She earned a Ph.D. in chemistry at the University of Michigan in 2018. Her doctoral dissertation focused on finite-temperature Green’s function methods, thermodynamics, spectra and quantum embedding.

Her subsequent published work includes hybrid quantum-classical approaches to molecular simulation. She is an Open Innovation Manager at Fujitsu Research of America. Previously, she conducted postdoctoral research at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and held research and technical account management roles at QC Ware.
