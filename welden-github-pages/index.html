Alicia R. Welden is a quantum chemist and computational scientist based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Her work connects electronic structure theory, quantum computing for chemistry, high-performance computing and emerging technology strategy.

Areas of expertise

Finite-temperature electronic structure

Green’s functions connect electronic structure with thermodynamic quantities and spectral properties. Welden’s doctoral research investigated finite-temperature methods, their numerical treatment and quantum embedding. Doctoral dissertation

Quantum computing for chemistry

Her collaborative research investigates how hybrid quantum-classical methods can address molecular interaction energies. Symmetry-adapted perturbation theory offers a way to separate contributions to intermolecular interactions. 2023 Chemical Science paper

Advanced computing and strategy

Her public writing examines practical performance metrics, utilization and the relationship between hardware and scientific workloads. Read published insights
